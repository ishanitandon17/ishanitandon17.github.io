About Us

Nestbox is a home decor brand being shaped around natural textures, thoughtful craftsmanship, and a quieter, more grounded way of living.

We are building a small-batch collection that brings together woven materials like water hyacinth and black clay pottery to create pieces that feel warm, tactile, and timeless.

Our aim is to celebrate honest materials, artisan-led making, and functional beauty for modern homes.
