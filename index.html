About Us

Nestbox is being built around a simple belief: home decor should be beautiful, useful, and honest about what it is made from.

We want to create products that celebrate natural woven textures, craft traditions, and transparent sourcing rather than over-designed mass production.

Our early ideas bring together materials like water hyacinth and black clay pottery to create pieces that feel grounded, warm, and real.
